👑♦️ THE KING OF DIAMONDS DOSSIER

⚖️ The Keynesian Beauty Contest

Originating from John Maynard Keynes' market analysis and featured in Alice in Borderland (Sixth Side Story), the game challenges players not to pick what they think is best, but what they anticipate the average participant believes the average will be.

📐 Core Mechanics

  • Every active player selects an integer from 0 to 100.
  • The exact average of all selections is multiplied by 0.8:
    Target = (Sum of Valid Selections / Valid Count) × 0.8
  • The player whose number is closest to this target wins the round.
  • All losing players receive -1 point.
  • When a player's score drops to -10 points, the balanced scale above them tips over, pouring lethal Aqua Regia (acid capable of dissolving gold) — causing GAME OVER.

🔓 Dynamic Rule Evolution (Upon Elimination)

1st Elimination (4 Left)

Clash / Invalidation Rule

If 2 or more players select the exact same number, their numbers are invalidated. They cannot win the round and both receive the penalty!

2nd Elimination (3 Left)

Exact Match Double Penalty

If any player chooses the exact calculated target (rounded), all losing players suffer -2 points instead of -1.

3rd Elimination (Final Duel)

The 0 vs 100 Paradox

When down to 2 players, if one player chooses 0, the opponent choosing 100 immediately overrules and wins. This creates a psychological Rock-Paper-Scissors cycle (0 beats 1, 1 beats 100, 100 beats 0)!

🧠 Character Game Theory Profiles

Keiichi Kuzuryū (King)

High-level game theorist. Employs recursive Level-$k$ iterations (converging to 0), but actively adapts when irrational players skew the distribution.

Hinako Daimon

Calculates variance and uses extreme outlier numbers (e.g. 100, 30) or clash traps to force errors on the rationalists.

Takashi Asuma

Traditional economist. Plays strict Level-1 / Level-2 logic ($50 \times 0.8 = 40 \to 32 \to 26$). Can be predictable.

Benzo Yashige

Operates on impulse and superstition. He ignores deep game theory, injecting chaotic noise that disrupts pure mathematical equilibrium.
